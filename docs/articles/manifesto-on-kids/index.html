Overall, having kids requires a change in mindset.

First, reset your mind. Throw away all your preconceived notions, hopes and dreams about having and raising kids. They are not true in many cases. More importantly, they are not helpful.

You choose to bring the kids into this world, you must own that decision and act like it. Do not complain because essentially you are complaining about yourself.

Trust that everything happens for a reason. Not only do you choose to have your kids, in many ways, the kids choose to have you in their lives because they believe in you. In their souls, they believe you will be a good parent and they are choosing a good family for themselves. Think of their coming into your family as an endorsement of you. This is you doing something great for humanity.

Second, life is different with kids, forever. Your friends without kids will not truly understand you, never. You cannot go back to your life without kids, ever. Your friends with kids will not utterly understand you because families have different circumstances. Accept it. This is an experience unique to you. This is an experiment unique to you. Some parents enjoy kids more than others. You might as well make the best out of this life, together with your kids. This life is yours. The kids are yours. Enjoy them, and enjoy all the goods and the "bads" that come with having them in your life. It is a choice and you can choose to enjoy your kids.

Raising kids is tough and exhausting. Keep this in mind and try to be prepared for the toughness and exhaustion every day. Setting this expectation as default will make your days a little easier.

Raising kids is like taking an emotional roller coaster, there are bound to be ups and downs. The downs will be much more painful than your pain threshold, but the ups will be much more joyful than you have ever envisioned, often unexpectedly. Your kids can enjoy a roller coaster, you should be able to too. Your mind just needs to be open to feel the pain as well as see the little moments of pure and simple beauty, tranquility and bliss. It is the thrill of a lifetime. Hold on to this if you have nothing else to hold on to when the "bad" time unavoidably comes.

Kids are your mirror. They are your making in front of you. If you are not happy with the kids, look into yourself first. If you are happy with the kids, give yourself a thumbs-up. Be self-reflective when it comes to the goods and "bads" of your kids. If your kids turn out well in the eyes of society, it is credit to you. If they don't, it is on you too. It is your responsibility. When you become impatient or lose your cool, always say to yourself, they are my mirror. Asking the kids to improve is asking yourself to improve. Take this to heart.

The process of raising children and helping them grow is also the process of helping yourself grow. Being able to keep upgrading yourself to be the best version of yourself and helping another human being to become the best version of himself or herself, is a gift that God only gives to good parents. Seize that gift. Cherish that gift. Take advantage of the journey of relearning and self-discovery.

The process is more about giving than receiving. Give your kids the gift of your presence and attention in their lives. Give without the expectation to receive, give especially in moments of disappointment, frustration, rejection and defeat. You will be able to say they are better because of you. Your kids will know they are loved.

Every kid is special in his or her own way. Your job is not to compare them to other kids, or your young self, or to project your own aspirations onto them, but to help them discover what makes them tick and unleash their potentials. If they have not discovered it yet, keep looking and keep helping them, with lots of encouragements and support.
