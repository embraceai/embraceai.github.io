When the Riemann Hypothesis Was Proved当黎曼猜想被证明當黎曼猜想被證明

An Age in Which Even the Greatest Experts Underestimate AI一个连顶级专家都低估了 AI 的时代一個連頂級專家都低估了 AI 的時代

In November 2026, mathematician Levent Alpöge and Claude produced a formal proof of the Riemann Hypothesis that was verified by the Lean and Mathlib communities. It came just 36 days after Terence Tao had speculated that a human–AI collaboration might not make a breakthrough on the problem until around 2050. The episode shows that even leading experts can underestimate the speed of AI progress. It also reveals a new model of research built on the collaboration between deep disciplinary expertise and frontier AI. Yet if the most powerful models and resources remain concentrated in a handful of institutions, research opportunities, doctoral training, the purpose of universities, and the value of ordinary people's work may all be reshaped.
